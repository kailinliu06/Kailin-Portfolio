I think of myself as someone who's equally comfortable with a regression model and a stack of flyers at a farmer's market. When I hit a complex problem, I don't just map it out — I look for the fastest way to actually move it forward.

That shows up across my work: building an attribute taxonomy out of 7,950 raw consumer reviews and running OLS/LASSO regressions to see what actually drives ratings; or standing alone on a Brooklyn sidewalk running an unscripted product test with dog owners because the app itself broke mid-internship and the experiment couldn't wait for a fix.

I'm also the person people hand ownership to when things get complicated. Over two years producing Chinese-language musical theater productions at UC Santa Barbara, I scaled a project from a 25-person, $2,500-budget show to a 36-person, $6,000+ production with a live band and three sold-out performances — recruiting across departments, negotiating licensing with original creators, and keeping a volunteer cast and crew on schedule under real constraints.

I'm currently targeting growth marketing, product marketing, and product management roles at tech companies and marketing agencies — anywhere that rewards turning ambiguity into a tested plan.
